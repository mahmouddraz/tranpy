# 12 Satellite checks for the owner (only the items still open)

A satellite or aerial image shows only what is physically there: conductors, towers, construction sites, cable trenches. It cannot tell whether a line is switched on. A check can therefore confirm "dismantled" or "under construction", but not "in service". Imagery dates differ between providers, so note the capture date of each image.

How to open the links:
- Google: `https://www.google.com/maps/@LAT,LON,17z/data=!3m1!1e3`
- Bing: `https://www.bing.com/maps?cp=LAT~LON&lvl=17&style=a`
- BayernAtlas: https://geoportal.bayern.de/bayernatlas/ (search the coordinates, Luftbild layer)
- Niedersachsen: https://www.geobasis.niedersachsen.de/ (DOP viewer) or the LGLN viewer https://www.lgln.niedersachsen.de/startseite/geodaten_karten/
- Hessen: https://www.geoportal.hessen.de/

The Hamburg and Schleswig-Holstein portals are not on the requested list; use Google or Bing for those.
